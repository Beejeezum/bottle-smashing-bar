# The Bottle Smashing Bar — design notes

## The premise

It is the last night of the last honest bar in town. The landlord, Mr. Pemberton, wants to turn the Smash Range into condos and will change the locks at 2 a.m. unless the jar on the bar is full. Every smash puts money in the jar. The regulars dare you for more.

Everyone in the bar is a living neon sign. So are you: the hand holding your glass is neon tube, and the restroom mirror shows the rest.

## The story so far

**Night one: the jar.**

1. Lou the bouncer waves you in out of the rain. Sal the bartender explains the jar, pours a free lager, and sends you to break something.
2. Each regular has a dare. Finishing one and telling them pays into the jar and gives a permanent perk. The clock on screen is the jar: it reads 2 AM when the jar is full.
3. Last call. Pemberton walks in and his personal collection is wheeled onto red pedestals in the range. Smash all five and he gives up the building, prints you a receipt, and takes a stool. Everything now pays double, and he has a dare of his own.

**Night two: the inspector.** Step outside and call it a night. The next evening Pemberton's cousin, a health inspector, is in the range reading the rules poster. He turns round without warning. Throw while he is watching and the jar is fined $150. Fill the jar to $2,500 to buy the permit.

## Drinking

- Sal builds each drink on the bar in front of you: the glass fills, slides over, and waits until you take it.
- Drinks have garnish: ice in the whiskey and water, foam on the stout, an olive in the martini, lime on the tequila.
- Drinking tips your head back and dims the room behind the glass. Strong drinks burn. Alcohol lands a few seconds after you swallow.
- Drunkenness stacks in layers: camera sway, double vision, a drifting walk, mouse-look that arrives late, random lurches, hiccups, slow heavy blinks. Past the top of the meter you black out and wake up at the bar.
- Drinks fill your bladder. Ignore it and Sal stops serving until you visit the restroom.
- Pretzels soften the next few drinks. Water sobers you up. The score multiplier rises with how drunk you are, so sobering up is a real trade.
- You can clink glasses with anyone, and tip Sal to bring happy hour round sooner.

## The range

- **Throws:** a fastball (click) or a high lob (right-click). Release in the green part of the wind-up ring for a perfect throw.
- **Row A, close:** bottle pyramids, a can stack, vases, gnomes, piggy banks, a fishbowl, lava lamps.
- **Moving:** the duck conveyor (it runs at the tempo of the jukebox), one golden duck, pop-up cardboard Pembertons, clay pigeons launched across the room.
- **Hanging:** frying pans, eight tuned chimes, a piñata, balloons, the disco ball, the chandelier.
- **Row B, on a riser:** televisions, melons, a cake, flamingos, the throne.
- **Back wall:** a shelf of teddies, gnomes and pottery, and three spinning bullseyes.
- **Events:** happy hour (free drinks, double points), Doug's Sixty (a minute of free empties with its own record), and the restock bell.
